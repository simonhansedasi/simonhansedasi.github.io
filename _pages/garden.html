--- title: "Soil Majick" permalink: /garden/ ---

Soil Majick is a garden simulation where the soil is the game. This free demo is the Pacific Northwest for one in-game year. Every crop eats nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium, nothing keeps the soil healthy except what you give back to it, and any two plants you cross can become a new species with its own name. The full game, with eight worlds from monsoon to Siberian frost, is coming to Steam.

Open it full screen. The first load downloads about 27 MB (a Python runtime), then it is cached. Click once to start so the browser allows sound. It is made for a desktop screen and mouse: a phone will run it, but the interface is small and tooltips need a hover. Saves stay in this browser.

How to play

  • Click a cell, then plant from the Cell tab. Hover a seed to see what it uses.
  • Time only moves when you press +1 day (or Space). Watch the bars: crops draw them down.
  • Rotation alone will not keep the soil fed. Compost your fruit to put back what the crop took, and keep nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium in proportion: a soil that is too lopsided locks plants out.
  • Plants that flower together cross. Seed a crossed fruit to keep the new strain. The Atlas tracks every species you find.
  • The goal: breed a hybrid line and carry it for four generations before the year is out.
