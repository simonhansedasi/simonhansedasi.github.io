--- title: "Soil Majick" permalink: /garden/ ---

Soil Majick is a garden simulation where the soil is the game. Every crop eats nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium, nothing keeps the soil healthy except what you give back to it, and any two plants you cross can become a new species with its own name. Eight worlds, from monsoon to Siberian frost, each with one goal.

The full game is coming to Steam. The free web demo is offline while the game is being finished.

How it plays

  • Time only moves when you press +1 day. Watch the bars: crops draw them down.
  • Rotation alone will not keep the soil fed. Compost your fruit to put back what the crop took, and keep nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium in proportion: a soil that is too lopsided locks plants out.
  • Plants that flower together cross. Seed a crossed fruit to keep the new strain; the Atlas tracks every species you find.
  • Every world has one goal that only a bred line can reach.
